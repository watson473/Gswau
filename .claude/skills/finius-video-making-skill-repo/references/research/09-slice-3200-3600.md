## C. Modern era + scandal: how the visuals shift

1. **Colour is the clock.** Pre-war rally footage is B&W (shots 1–8). The war starts in colour on the word "erupts" (shot 9). After that, colourised film carries the blitzkrieg. B&W returns only for iconic or "official" frames: Eiffel generals, Truman, VE crowds, Maxwell's early press events. Post-war London is warm Kodachrome. Maxwell in the 70s–80s is full-colour TV video with network bugs. So: **B&W = history being reported, colour = history being lived/now** [I].
2. **Source texture is left raw on purpose.** Burnt-in timecode on Truman, the CBS bug on the Maxwell footage, and a borrowed lower third on Greenslade are not cropped or cleaned. These "found document" marks signal authenticity in the modern era [M that they are kept; intent I].
3. **The 3D set becomes the home base of the London bank.** One Unreal room (dark wood, green damask, the "N M Rothschild & Sons" crest on a door, gilt frames, brass nameplates, stacked books, blind-slat light) is revisited 6 times between 2031 and 2162. It is always moving (orbit, dolly, rack focus, light sweep). It replaces the 1800s painted/engraved look with "institution" imagery.
4. **Character intro for the modern villain:** face first (animated colour gala photo on "a future media mogul"), name 6 s later. Then a music stop, black, and silence. Then a CV montage (B&W events → TV interview → dramatised older Maxwell in shadow). Then two witnesses on sync, then lifestyle colour footage (tux, yacht, wife). Finally the **photo of him in a frame on a desk inside the bank set, under blind-slat shadows**: the outsider literally placed inside the house.
5. **The power struggle is staged with objects and faces, not headlines or split screens.** No split screens, no newspaper headlines and no on-screen quotes appear in this slice [M]. The family feud is shown as engraved nameplates ("Jacob Rothschild", "Lord Victor Rothschild", "Sir Evelyn…") tracked past in the set, then three B&W portraits in gilt frames with a light beam raking across them. Conflict is implied by lighting (half-lit faces, slat shadows) and by sequence, not by graphics.
6. **The one piece of on-screen text is a fact card in a silent pause.** "…began banking with Rothschilds in the late 70s." White italic on black, mid-frame. It is used to plant a dated fact that the narration does not say, at the act turn.
7. **The creator appears as his own talking head** (shots 38, 41): a "talking heads deliver facts" beat filled by the narrator on camera. It sits in a B-roll sandwich (TH → London B-roll ×2 → TH).

## D. Patterns + recipes for our stack (HyperFrames 1920x1080 @30 fps, GSAP, AI stills/i2v, TTS Renee, whisper words, ffmpeg)

**D1. Montage acceleration for "everything falls" passages**
- Use 10–14 clips of 1.2–2.0 s (mean 1.6 s) for a list sentence such as "X, then Y, then Z fall". Cut on each place-name noun at +100 ±50 ms after the whisper word start.
- With stills: give each a 1.6 s i2v clip or a GSAP move of at least 8%/s. Example: `scale 1.00→1.12, x ±60px, ease:"none"`. Fast clips need fast moves.
- Put one impact SFX on the first noun of the list only (−8 dB under the voice peak). The rest stay dry.

**D2. Colour-era switch on a trigger word**
- Grade B&W (`filter: grayscale(1) contrast(1.15)`) up to the trigger word. Hard cut to full colour on it (+100–150 ms). Afterwards, return to B&W only for "official/iconic" frames.
- In ffmpeg for i2v clips: `hue=s=0,eq=contrast=1.15` for the B&W leg.

**D3. Slow push on a group/named photo while a roll call is read**
- Hold 6–8 s. GSAP `scale 1.00→1.17` over the shot (≈+3–4%/s), or a pull-out `1.16→1.00` (≈−2%/s), `ease:"power1.inOut"`.
- It must cover every name spoken. Do not cut mid-list.

**D4. Archive J-cut into a speech or document**
- Start the archival or quote audio 600 ms before the picture. Keep the source texture: a fake burnt-in timecode lower-left in monospace at ≈3% of frame height, white at 85% opacity. It should be honest: only use it on real archive or clearly styled recreations, never on fakes posing as real.

**D5. Breather after a climax**
- After a climactic line, run 10–12 s with no narration: 2–3 crowd/reaction clips of 4–5 s, music swelling to about −20 LUFS-M.
- Then change music cue on the cut into the new era. Restart the voice within 50 ms of the next cut.

**D6. Act-break reveal (black + silence)**
- Sequence: name line → music sustain 1.5–2.5 s → **hard music stop** → 0.75 s pure black (`#000`, 22 frames at 30 fps) with total silence (fade the music out over 30 ms, no ambience) → new cue → first B-roll.
- Show the face before the name: put the portrait up 5–6 s before the name is spoken.

**D7. Silent fact card**
- Fade the last shot to black over 0.95 s (`opacity 1→0, ease:"power1.in"`). Hold black 1.4 s.
- Text: single line, centred at y 50%, white `#fff`, italic humanist sans (Open Sans Italic 400) at 48–52 px (≈4.6% of H), letter-spacing 0. Fade in over 0.4 s (`ease:"power2.out"`, no slide). Hold ≥ words/3 s (use 3.3 s for 10 words; FINAiUS used 2.3 s, a bit fast). Dissolve out over 0.6 s into the next shot.
- Voice silent for the whole card (≈6 s). Music only, bed at about −30 LUFS-M. Put a low boom (sub hit) under the dissolve out.

**D8. Persistent 3D "institution" set (HyperFrames 2.5D version)**
- Build one room as layered AI stills: wall with damask, door with crest, desk with a framed photo, nameplates, books. Separate the layers via cut-outs at z depths −400/−200/0/+150 px with `perspective: 1200px`.
- Camera: a slow orbit or truck of 20–40 px/s on the foreground layer and 5–10 px/s on the background. Add a slight rack focus: `filter: blur(6px)→blur(0)` over 1.2 s on the hero layer.
- Light: a diagonal "venetian blind" gradient mask (5–7 stripes at 18% opacity, warm `#ffb070`, multiply/overlay) drifting 15 px/s, plus a light-sweep reveal (brightness 0.15→0.25 over 2.3 s).
- Re-use the same set at every return to that institution (6× in 2 minutes here). Each visit uses a new angle and 4–11 s holds.
- Names belong on objects (nameplate textures), not on overlay captions.

**D9. Photo-in-frame placement (character enters the institution)**
- Put the character's photo into a frame mesh/div on the desk of the set. Dolly in 1.00→1.25 over 5.3 s, then a closer 2.6 s shot. Blind-slat shadows fall across the face. Grade deep red-brown (`sepia(.4) saturate(1.3) hue-rotate(-10deg) brightness(.7)`).

**D10. Danger grade on a location**
- When the narration turns to peril for a place, render that building in a red-orange monochrome (`grayscale(1) sepia(1) saturate(4) hue-rotate(-25deg)`), tilt it 8–12° (dutch), and dolly up to its signage over 5–6 s.

**D11. Witness / interview grammar**
- Show 2.5 s of B-roll of the witness before they speak. Then wide with a lower third for the whole shot (4 s).
