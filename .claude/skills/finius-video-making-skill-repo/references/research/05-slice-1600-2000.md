# 05: Slice 16:00–20:00 (959.75–1209 s), sheets 032–039

Source: FINAiUS "Empire of Shadows". This slice holds the **exact midpoint of the film** (39:28 / 2 = 1184 s) and the **only sponsor read in it** (Brilliant, 1091.3–1178 s).

Method: cuts come from a 24 fps frame-difference pass at 96x54 grey (it matched work/cuts.txt and added the dissolves). I also pulled start, middle and end frames for every shot. Text timing comes from pixel thresholds on full-res frames. Audio comes from 0.5 s RMS, a 30–85 Hz "bass" band, a >6 kHz band, spectral flux, spectrograms and autocorrelation tempo. Word times are from the VTT; the last word before a pause is often stretched, so I cross-checked those against work/transcript.txt.
**M** = measured, **I** = inferred.

Chapters inside the slice (from meta): 953 "French Bank Collapses", 1011 "Lionel Rothschild Enters Government", 1181 "The Rise of the House of Morgan". The sponsor sits **inside** the 1011 chapter, at its tail, with no chapter of its own.

**Shot lengths (story part, 959.75–1091.3, 26 shots)**: mean 5.06 s, median 5.5 s, min 1.67 s, max 8.12 s.
- Distribution: <3 s ×6 (all FILM shots in the parliament montage), 3–6 s ×11, 6–8.2 s ×9.

## C. THE MIDPOINT (1077–1190 s)

The midpoint of the film is 1184 s. The sponsor ends at 1178 s and the new act starts at 1181 s, so **he places the single mid-film sponsor so that its exit is the midpoint**. The sequence, measured:

1. **Close the act on a win.** Lionel is seated, then becomes patriarch. Portraits of him are shown three times in rising "status": a B/W engraving, then a colourised close-up, then a gilt frame in a gallery room.
2. **Re-hook with dramatic irony** (1070–1085): "what Lionel doesn't realize…" plus "a new banking empire emerges". This is the classic "but this was only the beginning" form. The picture plants the payoff early: **a dark corridor reveals a brass door sign of the rival firm** at the end of a slow truck, before the name is spoken. The music builds for about 15 s.
3. **Hard stop.** The music cuts to near-silence for about 0.6 s (1085.0–1085.9). This is the pacing reset; the viewer notices the change.
4. **Thematic bridge** (1086–1091, ~5 s). The story's own theme (the family won by information) is turned into the ad's premise (learning is easy now). There is no "but first, a word from our sponsor". The cut into the ad is the biggest brightness jump in the slice: a dark red room becomes white daylight stock.
5. **Sponsor, 87 s.** The structure: problem/premise → product shot → the creator on camera (4 TH inserts of 3–6 s, so the face carries trust) → UI demos cut every 1–1.5 s → a personal use case (crypto, matched to a finance audience) → a feature list with a demo beat per claim → URL kinetic type → offer card → logo → thanks.
   - It has a distinct visual world (bright, white, vignetted, modern) and a pulsed bed. **The ad is visually quarantined from the dark story palette**, so the return reads as "back to the film".
6. **Palate cleanser**: 3 s of black and fading audio, ending in true silence.
7. **Cold re-entry into the paid-off cliffhanger** (1181): a new chapter and a heavier new cue on the fade-up. The first word names the rival promised before the ad ("Junius Morgan"). We are in a dark 3D room, then at a door plaque (echoing the J.S. Morgan sign from step 2), then in sunlit film footage of the new man.
   - **The visual rhyme (door sign before the ad, door plaque after it) stitches across the break.**
   - Within 18 s he adds a juicy character-sketch quote with a book-cover and author lower third (authority plus colour).

Other re-capture devices in the slice:
- "But it doesn't stop there" (1000.4) after a 5 s music-only breath.
- **Creator TH asides** (974, 1018): a face and a casual opinion line break the archival rhythm every ~45 s.
- The **silent text card** (1055–1058.6): a sudden absence of voice, then a boom.

**How politics/Parliament is visualised:**
- **Establish the institution with feature-film footage.** A high-angle symmetrical chamber wide, then crowd, then seated members, then the bewigged officials: 4 shots of 1.7–2.7 s, the fastest cutting in the slice. Parliament = crowd energy.
- **Archive engraving of the Commons with a fast pan and zoom** for the historical fact (the bill passed).
- **Attribution lower third on a 3D desk photo of the author** while the electoral fact is read: "this is sourced".
- **Symbols carry the procedural conflict**: the **golden mace** for "There is a problem" and the **Bible with a cross** for the oath. Objects stand in for the abstract rules.
- **Resolution as silent black text**: dry legal facts get one line of type and no voice.
- **Re-entry with a painting of the Commons hung in a 3D gallery**, next to the protagonist's portrait. The institution and the man share a wall, which is the visual statement of "he's in".

## D. PATTERNS + RECIPES (HyperFrames / GSAP, 1920x1080 @ 30 fps)

1. **3D-room portrait push (fake Unreal).**
   - Layers: back wall texture (damask/green, blurred 2 px); framed AI still (gilt frame PNG, 45–55 % frame height); foreground chandelier/lamp PNG blurred 12–18 px; dust-mote particles (20–40 dots, 2–4 px, opacity 0.3–0.6, drifting 10–20 px/s).
   - Camera: scale the world 1.00→1.20 over the shot (**3 %/s**, `power1.inOut`). Move the foreground 2.5x faster than the wall (parallax), and pan y −20 px.
   - Shot length 5–7 s. Never hold one under 4 s with a static camera.

2. **Lateral truck with a foreground wipe.** Wall and frame x +0→−60 px. The foreground object x +0→−400 px leaves frame by about 60 % of the shot. Scale constant. 6–7 s.

3. **Rack-focus transition.** Shot starts with the subject blurred 10 px and the foreground sharp; animate subject blur 10→0 and foreground 0→14 px over 1.2 s (`power2.inOut`), starting 0.3 s in. For an exit, reverse it in the last 0.8 s and cut on full blur (shot #2's defocus exit).

4. **Prop-framed interview.** Place an external quote/clip (or an AI-generated "expert" clip, disclosed) inside a laptop or monitor screen in the 3D room. Add a diagonal light streak (a white linear gradient, opacity 0.25, `mix-blend: screen`, drifting 40 px/s). Dolly out and left 5 %, and end with the defocus exit. Let the clip's audio lead the cut by 0.4 s (J-cut).

5. **Author/source lower third.** Template as measured: x 10 % W; bottom at 90 % H; name 6.3 % H bold, white; label 3.5 % H light, above.
   - In: the name `x:-330→0, filter: blur(8px)→0, opacity 0→1, 0.5 s, power3.out`, starting 0.35 s after the cut; the label follows +0.33 s.
   - Hold ≥3 s. Out: the label first, then the name `x:0→-60, blur 0→8, opacity→0, 0.25 s, power2.in`, ending ≥0.5 s before the cut.
   - Use it only while a sourced passage is read, over a background that shows the author or the book (book cover, desk photo).

6. **Silent black fact card.** Picture fades to black 0.5 s; hold black 0.25 s; text (centred, 5.2 % H regular, white, max one line ~60 chars) fades in 0.5 s; hold = words × 0.30 s; fade out 0.5 s; next picture fades up 0.5 s.
   - **Mute the narration**: the music drops about 2 dB, and place a low boom/impact (-12 dBFS peak, 30–80 Hz) at the moment the next picture starts fading up.
   - The next narration sentence starts with the fade-up (+0.3 s).
   - Use it once per act for a resolution/time-jump.

7. **Parliament/institution montage.** 4 shots of 1.7–2.7 s, in the order: wide symmetrical high-angle establishing, crowd mid, seated profile, authority figures. AI stills plus image-to-video 3 s clips (or still plus 6 % push and handheld noise at ±3 px, 0.5 Hz). Darken the corners with a vignette (edges −35 %).
