## C. How the death and the handover of power are staged

**Death (Nathan), 694–749, ~55 s, nine shots:**
1. **Foreshadow first.** A line about the "ultimate enemy" plays over B&W funeral footage *before* the death is stated. The image runs ahead of the words.
2. **Hollow out the sound.** Narration stops for 8.4 s. Music drops ~7 LU, then a 1 s bass hole falls under a **frozen** final frame (empty cemetery) that fades to black in 0.38 s.
3. **Enter a dark private room.** The first new image is an *empty chair* in a red-brown room (L16), with a slow truck that reveals a framed portrait. Absence is shown before the person.
4. **Then the stated fact, in near-stillness.** The deathbed shot has the lowest luma (L13) and lowest motion of the slice and holds 6.1 s. The narration gives a date, no adjectives.
5. **Name and age land on a memorial portrait,** lamp-lit amber (L≈85, warm+60). The colour swings from cold/black to warm, like a candle at a wake.
6. **Release valve:** the presenter on camera for an 11 s human aside (his mother outlived him). This is the longest shot, and it resets attention.
7. **Public reaction:** a newspaper quote over B&W mourners, then the only cold-blue shot (coffin), then a 3D **citation prop** (book + author photo + lower third) with an accelerating push-in that "enters" the source.

**Handover (James), 749–816:**
- A **geography of power** before any face. London bank interior, then London plaque, then **Paris plaque** (the warmest frame so far) under "they need a new leader". The house is introduced before the heir.
- The heir's **first appearance is full length**: a standing portrait hung in a 3D room (9.3 s, slow truck) exactly under "commander-in-chief". Then a **tight close-up** of the same painting for the "pressure" line. **Full figure = rank; close-up = inner state.**
- The character description (from a book quote) plays over an **11.4 s ECU zoom-out** on a painted face, draining warm+40→+9 and L32→17. That is the slowest, darkest single move, saved for character.
- The heir is later **re-hung next to his industry**: the portrait shares the wall with a framed railway photo (843). Portraits recur as visual anchors at the start of each new act (955).

**Money and industry visuals:**
- **Railroads:** the bright exterior break after 3+ minutes of dark interiors (L90–100, aerial). Archive is shown three ways: colourised film, a 3D-framed photo that pulls back, and a locomotive in a shed. The section **ends on spectacle and music**: a 10.5 s music-only window with steam that whites out the frame and becomes the transition.
- **Profit and finance:** recurring gold Paris plaque (warm+75–80, L110+). Gold equals the bank, every time it returns (4 appearances in 4 minutes).
- **Income disparity:** a hard **grade flip** from gold/warm to desaturated grey (sat 4, L32) at the steam-whiteout cut, plus a music cue flip to a drone. Literal hunger images (hands reaching for bread, hooded women). Then a satirical caricature (financiers carving a map) as the bridge into an expert's opinion (J-cut).
- **Crisis:** the riot as an animated engraving, then orange fire-tinted film. A fade to black (0.5 s + 0.29 s black) and a riser lead to the **bank's plaque, then the bank building on fire** (a visual metaphor for "breaking point").

## D. Patterns + recipes (HyperFrames 1920×1080 @30 fps, GSAP, AI stills, Kling, OmniVoice "Renee", whisper, ffmpeg)

1. **Death beat ("hollow then still")**
   - Line N: foreshadowing sentence over 2 B&W stills (2.5–3.5 s each, slow 1.00→1.04 scale, `power1.inOut`).
   - Then **8 s with no narration**. Duck music −6 to −7 LU (to ~−25 LUFS), and leave a **1.0 s bass hole**: `highpass=f=180` automated over that second, or fade out the music's low stem.
   - The last still freezes (no scale) for 0.7 s, then `opacity 1→0` over 0.38 s (`power2.in`) to black.
   - Cut up to a dark interior AI still (target mean luma ≈15–30/255, warm grade) with a slow x-truck of 3–4% W over 4 s.
   - Next, the subject shot nearly still (scale 1.00→1.015 over 6 s). Narration resumes **0.45 s after the cut** with a bare date-and-fact sentence.
   - Then a lamp-lit portrait at warm grade (luma ~80) for the name and age.
2. **Human aside after a heavy beat.** Insert a 8–11 s "presenter" or talking-head beat. For us: Renee VO over a neutral, mid-luma (~65) locked-off card or set with a gentle 1.00→1.02 push. It resets attention after a death or reveal.
3. **Citation shot (source as prop).**
   - AI still of the book on a desk plus a framed author photo; we generate generic props and never copy real covers.
   - GSAP push on the scene container: `scale 1→2, x:-8%, duration 9, ease "power2.in"` (accelerating), ending as the photo fills the frame. Cut right after.
   - Lower third:
     - type: role in light 300 weight at 36–40 px; name in semibold 600 at 62 px (5.7% FH); white; no plate; `text-shadow 0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.6)`; left 10% W, baseline 89.6% H;
     - in: start **0.7 s after the cut**, name `x:-190→0, opacity 0→1, filter blur(8px)→0, 0.55 s power3.out`; role follows **+0.15 s**;
     - hold 3.6 s;
     - out: `x:0→-120, blur 0→8, opacity→0, 0.33 s power2.in`.
4. **Cut-on-the-stressed-word.** Using whisper word times, place cuts **40–100 ms before** the onset of the name, date or key noun inside a phrase (target 2 frames at 30 fps = 67 ms). A scene-opening cut after a pause goes **0.4–1.0 s before** the first word. Don't put more than 1 in 5 cuts in sentence-end pauses.
5. **Shot length budget.** Target median 5 s, IQR 3.8–7.2 s, min 2 s. Allow 9–11.5 s only for (a) a slow push or pull at ≤0.25× scale per second, (b) a talking head, or (c) a citation shot. Character and death sections run ~25% longer per shot (6.6 s) than exposition (5.1 s).
6. **Portrait grammar for a power shift.** First show the institution: 2–3 plaque/sign shots (AI 3D-style renders of our own invented signage), with the new seat of power as the warmest frame (R−B ≈ +70). Then the heir full length in a framed portrait in a room, then a close-up of the same image.
