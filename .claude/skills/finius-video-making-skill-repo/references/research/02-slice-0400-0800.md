## D. PATTERNS + RECIPES (for HyperFrames, 1920x1080, 30 fps)

Camera-move estimates (brute-force zoom/shift match between first and last frame) are in D10.

### D1. Clause-onset cut
- Snap every cut to `wordStart(firstWordOfClause) − 130 ms` (allowed window −500…0 ms). With whisper word times: for each beat, `cutFrame = round((t_word − 0.13) * 30)`.
- For list sentences ("management, judgment, foresight"), cut **on** the stressed noun at −0…−80 ms. Use one image per item.
- At a full stop followed by a pause of 1.5 s or more, cut **inside the pause** about 40 % of the way through it. Don't cut on the next word.

### D2. Montage pace vs intimacy
- Context/"world" montage (empire, industry, city): **3–4 s shots**, mostly literal stock/film/AI, each shot one clause.
- Character/story stretches: **5–10 s shots** in 3D or AI close-ups with continuous slow moves.
- Death/turning point: **7–14 s**, very dark (mean luma 10–35 of 255).
- Insert one **0.6–0.7 s accent shot** per ~60 s (POV whip, citation flash) to break the rhythm.

### D3. Date/place stamp ("1800", "Manchester")
- Font: blackletter (UnifrakturMaguntia / Pirata One) for places, or a high-contrast old-style serif for years. Colour `#F8D7BE`. Glyph height **18–19 % of frame (≈200 px)**, centred at x 50 %, y 43–45 %. Optional `text-shadow: 0 4px 18px rgba(0,0,0,.45)`.
- Enter **0.8–1.9 s after the shot's cut**. Either a hard pop (1 frame), or an "ink reveal": `clip-path`/mask wipe plus opacity 0→1 over **4 frames (≈130 ms)**, `ease: "power2.out"`.
- Hold to the **end of the shot** (2.4–3.2 s); it leaves on the hard cut, with no out-animation.
- Best placed where the VO is **silent** or ≈2 s before the matching word.

### D4. Historian/source citation ("framed photo on a desk" + name lower third)
- Scene: a dark desk set. A gilt picture frame (CSS border image or a PNG frame) holds the author's photo **[use our own licensed or AI-generic "scholar" portrait; never a real person's photo without rights]**, plus the book cover, an open blank book and blurred foreground props.
- Fake depth in HyperFrames: 3–4 layers (blurred bokeh foreground `filter: blur(12px)`, the frame, a blurred background). Parallax over 6–8 s: foreground x −60 px, frame x −20 px with scale 1.00→1.06, background x −8 px, `ease: "sine.inOut"`.
- Open with a **rack focus**: the frame layer goes `blur(14px)→0` over **1.5 s**.
- Lower third: name only, **white bold humanist sans** (Open Sans 700), `letter-spacing: .05em`, **font-size ≈56 px (5.2 % H)**, left 200 px, baseline at 84 % H, **no background bar**. In: `x: -280 → 0, opacity 0→1, filter blur(8px)→0`, **0.7 s `expo.out`**, 1.2 s after the cut. Hold 3 s. Out: opacity →0 in **0.35 s `power1.in`**, ≥1.4 s before the cut.
- Re-use: return to the same desk set each time the script quotes or paraphrases that source (3 times in 80 s here). Include a 0.6 s "flash" of it at the end of a long quote.

### D5. Foreshadow text card
- Background `#000`. Text: one short sentence, condensed semibold sans (Open Sans Condensed 600 / Barlow Condensed 600), colour `#E8D0C0`, **font-size ≈54 px (5 % H)**, centred, max width 80 %.
- Timeline: **hard cut to black** (picture and the music's high end drop together; keep the low drone), then 0.8 s of black, a **0.5 s linear fade-in**, hold **≈0.2 s × words (min 1.9 s)**, a **0.55 s linear fade-out**, and 0.4 s of black before a hard cut to an establishing shot.
- No VO over it. Audio: low-pass the bed to ~250 Hz (or crossfade to a drone stem) during the black, then restore it at the next cut, optionally with a soft accent SFX (−24 dB).
- Use it at most once per 10 minutes, for a death or a reversal.

### D6. Chapter breath (no title card)
- End the chapter's last VO sentence, then leave a **5–11 s VO gap**.
- Pick the punctuation by stakes:
  - (a) Music swell: +6–7 dB on the bed for the gap (automate from −30 to −23 LUFS, 1 s ramps).
  - (b) Name reveal → silence → place stamp (D3) → a slow intimate shot; the VO returns **1.4 s** after that shot's cut.
  - (c) Black + text card (D5).
  - (d) Suck-out → impact: duck the bed to −40 dB / high-pass >200 Hz over 1.5 s, hold 3–4 s, place **one black frame (33 ms)**, then cut on a boom SFX (peak −6 dBFS, +20 dB over the bed). Follow with a **white cloud/smoke whiteout** (an overlay layer opacity 1→0 over 1.5 s) into the next scene.

### D7. Whiteout transition (steam, smoke, cloud)
- At the cut, overlay a white smoke/cloud video or PNG sequence (or a radial-gradient white layer) at full opacity. Decay it to 0 over **1.2–1.5 s**, `ease: "power2.out"`, while the new shot scales 1.08→1.00 underneath.
- Use it when the narration jumps a topic (industry, or war → geography). The luma pattern measured: 104 → 203 (peak) → 72 within 1.3 s.

### D8. Host on camera as a "thesis anchor"
- He appears 3 times in 4 min, each **5–9.5 s**, always speaking a **claim or thesis line**, never a transition. He is lit warm (luma ≈64) in an MCU against a plain wall with one art print.
- For us: use Renee as an avatar or a stylised presenter card only if we have one. Otherwise use a **"pull-quote card"** in the same slot: the narration continues while the key sentence types on.
- Duck the music's low end **−8 dB below 150 Hz** during these shots (measured: −10 to −15 dB in the low band).

### D9. Dark 3D "story" shots with slow camera (character, deathbed, coins)
- When we lack 3D: take an AI still (low-key, 1–2 light sources, luma mean 10–35/255) and give it a **continuous move for the whole 6–14 s**. Use one of these:
  - **Push** scale 1.00→1.10 over 8 s, `sine.inOut`
  - **Lateral track** x 0→−120 px with a 1.12 base scale over 10 s
  - **Fake orbit**: 2 layers (subject cut-out plus background) with opposite x drift of ±40 px, plus a `rotateY` of 4–6° on the subject plane with `perspective: 1200px`
  - **Rack focus**: background blur 0→10 px while the foreground goes 10→0 px over 1.5 s at the start
- A slow **exposure fall** across a long shot (luma 21→12 or 50→33) works as a "closing in" mood cue: animate a black overlay's opacity 0→0.35 over the shot.
- Use image-to-video (Kling) for shots needing inner motion: flags, water, smoke, walking characters. Keep the camera locked in those (AI ships, shot #4).

### D11. Map grammar
- Data map (flat): navy-to-purple gradient sea, white land, crimson territories. A slow drift plus a **warm light-leak bloom** in the last 1 s (a radial-gradient `#FFB347` layer, opacity 0→0.6, `mix-blend-mode: screen`).
- 3D relief flyover with a world-anchored label: blackletter mint `#B3DED5` with a glow (`text-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(179,222,213,.7)`), ≈10 % H. Pass through cloud layers (2–3 PNG cloud planes moving toward the camera at different speeds) for the in/out wipes.

### D12. Grade/finish
- No letterbox in this slice. Mild vignette on the 3D and AI shots. Archival stills are kept in their native colour (pastel engraving, sepia painting, B&W film with grain/flicker); they are **not** unified to one grade.
- Overall the slice alternates **bright archival/stock (luma 100–200)** for context with **near-black 3D (luma 6–35)** for character. That brightness alternation is itself a rhythm device: context is bright, character is dark.
