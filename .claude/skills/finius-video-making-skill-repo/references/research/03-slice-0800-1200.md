## C. How war and money flows are visualised

1. **Geography as a 3D flyover, not a flat map.** Unreal satellite-textured terrain, with the camera at about 200–500 m and a 20–30° downward tilt, flying fast with motion blur. Cities are marked by **waving period flags on poles** (Kingdom of Naples, Napoleonic tricolour with eagle, Union Jack) plus a **blackletter label at the flag foot, tinted toward the flag's colour**. Moving between cities uses two diegetic transitions: **rising through a cloud layer** (about 1.1 s fully white-grey, so the cut hides inside the cloud) and **a vertical whip into blue sky** (about 1 s, with 1.2 s of audio silence).
2. **The five-city network as a physical object on the map.** Each house is a **small 3D bank building** (white/gilt neoclassical block, scaled very large relative to the terrain). They are connected by a **full mesh of thick cream "rope" lines** that sag slightly as catenary-like arcs; every city links to every other, not just a hub-and-spoke from Frankfurt. The labels use the same blackletter, white and extruded. The lines are **already drawn when the shot opens**: there is no draw-on animation. Motion comes from one continuous **diagonal crane** from London toward Vienna (about 1 building spacing per 2 s). Lines run out of frame toward the fifth city, so the network reads as larger than the frame. The shot is 5.6 s, cut 0.39 s before "network". He relies on the 3D object for meaning, with no arrows or data.
3. **Money flow as a journey.** Wellington's gold is a **glowing ship on a night globe leaving a dotted light trail** (dots evenly spaced, white glow, about 0.6% frame-height radius [I]). The camera follows close for 7 s, then pulls back in 1.35 s to reveal the **whole dotted route** (India → Cape → Britain) on a spinning globe. This is the only "arrow" device in the slice, and the dots form the arrow.
4. **War as feature-film fragments.** Battles are never mapped with troop arrows. Instead: a 9 s smoky battlefield with a French tricolour (establishing), riflemen and naval clips (2–3.5 s), a colourised period illustration with a push, and a **burst montage of 0.5–1.3 s cavalry/volley shots** with whip blurs after a black flash. Colour is washed out: teal battlefields and foggy white.
5. **Political maps (2D)** appear twice (4.5 s and 6 s). They are flat bright-colour period maps with slow pushes. One fades up from dark. They carry no added overlays (no highlight, no arrows); the map simply sits under the line about Europe/foreign exchange.
6. **Money imagery** is literal and tactile: coins on a yellow table (stock), coins scattered on the 3D desk, a box placed on a table (drama clip), a handwritten letter quoting £800,000. **No numerals on screen.**
7. **Speed and information:** the map fades up from dark on "speed is king", then a stock-exchange engraving, then horses and a pigeon. Each is a literal noun insert.

## D. Patterns and recipes (for HyperFrames 1920x1080 @30 fps, Renee TTS, whisper timing, ffmpeg mix)

**R1. Cut timing.** For each B-roll shot, set `in = wordOnset(keyword or sentence start) − 0.38 s` (clamp 0.2–0.6 s). Literal noun inserts may land +0.25 s after the noun. Never cut mid-word: snap to the nearest whisper gap of 120 ms or more before the target word.

**R2. Expert/talking-head J-cut.** Start the expert audio 1.5–5 s (up to 10 s for a long quote) before showing the face; cover the lead with matching B-roll. Duck the music −10 dB (or mute the bass) under experts; bring it back at the next narrator chapter line.

**R3. Lower third (copy of the measured spec).** Name: Open Sans 700, `font-size` so cap height = 48 px (≈ 66 px font size), tracking 0.04em, #FFFFFF, `text-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.45)`, left 10.4%, baseline 84–90%. Optional role line above: 15 px caps, weight 400, tracking 0.12em, opacity 0.8, 8 px gap. GSAP: `from({x:-190, opacity:0}, {duration:0.5, ease:"power3.out"})` starting 0.1–0.9 s after the face cut; hold 3.5 s; `to({opacity:0, duration:0.3})`. Total about 4 s. Allow it to overrun the next cut by up to 0.4 s.

**R4. City hop, a 2.5D version of the Unreal flyover.** Layer stack per scene: satellite still of the region (AI or NASA Blue Marble crop, 3840 px) inside a container with `perspective:1200px`, plane `rotateX(62deg)`. Camera = GSAP on plane `translateY` −35% and `scale` 1→1.25 over 3 s, ease "none", plus `filter: blur(2px)` on a duplicated foreground strip for motion blur. Flag = small looping MP4/webm (or CSS skew-wave) on a pole, billboarded (counter-rotate X). Label in **UnifrakturMaguntia** (Google Fonts), 6–7% of frame height, coloured toward the flag (mix 70% white + 30% flag colour), shadow 0 4px 12px #0008. Pop the label in with `scale 0.6→1, opacity 0→1, 0.35 s back.out(1.6)` at `T(cityName) − 0.3 s`.
Transitions: (a) **cloud pass**: 3 cloud PNG layers scale 1→2.6 and opacity 0→1→0 over 1.1 s (power1.inOut) with a hard scene switch at the midpoint (fully covered at 0.45–0.65 s). (b) **Whip-up**: `translateY 0→+120%` with `blur 0→24px` over 0.45 s (power2.in), a 0.1 s solid deep blue (#1d4f8f), then the new scene in from −120% (power2.out, 0.45 s). **Mute VO and music for 1.0–1.2 s across the whip** (ffmpeg `volume=enable='between(t,a,b)':volume=0` with 60 ms fades) instead of adding a whoosh.

**R5. Network-of-cities map.** SVG over a darkened satellite still, the plane tilted as in R4. Nodes: 5 AI-generated isometric bank-building PNGs (transparent, about 9% frame height). Edges: **full mesh** (5 nodes = 10 cubic paths), each control point lifted 6–10% for a sag/arc, `stroke:#EFE6CF; stroke-width:7px; stroke-linecap:round`, plus a second blurred stroke at 40% opacity for glow. Labels: UnifrakturMaguntia, white, 7–8% height, under each building. He shows the mesh already drawn. For our version, keep the shot short (5–6 s) and either keep it pre-drawn or, if we want the build, draw edges with `strokeDashoffset` stagger 0.12 s, 0.6 s each, finishing **before** the keyword. Camera: one continuous diagonal dolly (translate the plane by about 1 node spacing per 2 s, ease "sine.inOut"), with 1–2 edges running off-frame to imply scale. Cut in at `T("network") − 0.4 s`.

**R6. Money route (globe or map + ship + dotted trail).** Dark-ocean globe (three.js sphere with an Earth texture in HyperFrames, or a flat equirectangular map with a slight pan if 3D is too costly). Ship icon: golden glow PNG (`drop-shadow(0 0 12px #ffcf7a)`). Trail: circles r = 6 px, every 26 px along the path, white with a `0 0 10px #fff` glow, revealed in sync with the ship (GSAP MotionPath + stagger). Shot plan, 12 s total: 7 s follow (camera locked to the ship, scale 2.2), **1.3 s pull-back to scale 1 (power3.inOut)**, 4 s slow rotate (6–10° total). Start the shot 0–0.1 s before the first quote word.

**R7. Quote on a document.** Paper texture plane, CSS `rotateX(38deg) rotateZ(−6deg)`, top-left warm key light (radial gradient overlay), vignette to black on the far edge. Handwriting font (Caveat / Homemade Apple), line height 3.5% of frame height, the full quote written out. Hold: one 2.5–3 s wide shot of the desk, then a 12–15 s close-up with a lateral slide of 8% of frame width + scale 1→1.06 (ease "none"). The VO reads the text verbatim; no highlight is needed.

**R8. Battle/war burst.** After a 2.5–3 s music-only breath: 2 black frames (67 ms at 30 fps), the first clip rising from black over 0.17 s, then **6–7 clips at 0.5–1.3 s each** (mean 0.85 s), with 1–2 whip-blur joins (`blur 0→18px` + `x ±15%`, 0.2 s). Grade: desaturate 30%, lift blacks, a teal/white fog look. Resume VO on the 2nd–3rd clip. Use AI-video or licensed clips only (we do not use film clips).

**R9. Cannon/flash transition.** Full-white solid for 0.67 s (start at 100% white at the cut), then the next image beneath with the white fading 1→0 over 0.5 s (power2.out). Tint the incoming shot lavender (#9a9cff multiply at 35%). He leaves it silent; for us, an optional low boom at −18 dB.

**R10. Still image rule.** Every still (portrait, illustration, engraving, map) gets a push of 1.04–1.10x across its duration (ease "none" or "sine.inOut") and stays ≤ 6 s. 3D/animated shots may run 8–15 s only if the camera keeps moving. Talking heads may run 11–14 s.

**R11. Death/turn beat (about every 3 min).** 1–2 s before the turn: gate the music bed in a rhythmic stutter (100 ms mutes every 0.6 s for 2 s, via ffmpeg `volume=enable='lt(mod(t-T0,0.6),0.1)':volume=0`). Narrator line ending on the hook; foreshadow visuals (a funeral) **before** the death is stated. Then **8 s with no VO**: crowd shot → the same place empty (hard cut, +15% exposure) → a 0.4 s fade to black with **1 s of near-silence** → a new, sparser cue under a dark interior that brightens over 4 s (exposure ramp 16→39 luma, about +0.6 stop). VO returns with the date, 0.5 s after the next cut.

**R12. Dip to black between segments.** Cut hard to black for 1.4 s; start the narrator's next line 0.6 s into the black; cut (not fade) into the next picture.

**R13. No free-floating text graphics.** In this stretch every word on screen lives inside the world (flags, signs, letters, map labels) or is a name lower third. For our style: put numbers such as "8%" or "£800,000" on in-world props (ledger page, coin stack label, document) rather than on a floating card, unless we deliberately want our own card style.
