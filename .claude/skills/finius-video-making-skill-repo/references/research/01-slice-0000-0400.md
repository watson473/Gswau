## D. PATTERNS + RECIPES (for our tools: HyperFrames 1920x1080 @30 fps, AI stills, image-to-video, TTS, ffmpeg, SFX, music beds)

1. **Cold-open crane reveal (one 20 s shot).** Build a 3D-looking still pair: (a) silhouettes before a columned facade at night, backlit; (b) an emblem on the same facade. HyperFrames: stack both layers. Layer A: `scale 1.00→1.06` over 10.5 s (`none`/linear), then `y: 0→+60%` over 4 s (`power2.inOut`) to fake the tilt-up. Layer B: start `y:-40%, opacity 0`, animate to `y:0, opacity 1` over 3 s (`power2.out`), starting at 11.5 s. Add a brightness ramp `filter: brightness(0.35→1)` over 14-19 s. Or generate it with image-to-video: "slow crane up from silhouettes to emblem, 10 s", and use 2 clips with a 1.5 s cross-dissolve hidden inside the move.
2. **Music drop → boom on the "rise" line.** Mute the bed (−∞) for 0.6-0.9 s right after the darkest clause, then hit an impact (sub boom, −6 dBFS peak) together with a full-level cue entry on the turn word. Bed under the hook: −25 dB relative to the voice. After the boom: −12 dB.
3. **Geography preview map.** A 5-6 s shot of a dark satellite map with small 3D landmarks + glowing connector lines, with an accelerating push `scale 1.00→1.10, ease power2.in`. Place it right after the hook sentence as a table of contents.
4. **Light-burst transition (0.4 s).** Overlay a white light-ray/leak plate (`mix-blend-mode: screen`): opacity 0→1 in 0.08 s, hold 0.1 s, →0 in 0.25 s. Hard-cut the picture underneath at the peak. SFX: a short reverse-cymbal/whoosh ending at the cut, −14 dB.
5. **Posterised metaphor "title image".** Process an archival photo with ffmpeg `eq=contrast=2.2:saturation=0, curves` (2-tone cream #E8E2CF / black), add `noise=alls=18:allf=t`, plus a 1-2 px RGB split at the edges. Composite an AI-made metaphor element (roots, globe, chains) in the lower half. Pull-out `scale 1.25→1.00` over 10 s, `power2.out`, then hold with ±1% drift. Dim −30% over the last 3 s.
6. **Black + boom act break.** Hard cut to pure black for 2.5-3.0 s. Put an impact 0.25-0.3 s *after* the cut, then let it ring out ~2.3 s to silence. Bring the next cue up from silence with the first frame (fade 0.9 s, to −12 dB under the voice).
7. **Place/date slate.** Hard text pop with no animation, 0.5 s after the shot starts. Hold 3.25 s, hard off, then 0.8 s clean plate before the cut. Font: an ultra-condensed Didone (free stand-ins: "Bodoni Moda" condensed via `transform: scaleX(0.55)`, or "Six Caps"/"Abril Fatface" compressed). Two centred lines, cap height 21% of frame height, colour #7DB9FF, no shadow. Use it once per chapter or location, *before* the narrator names the place.
8. **"Every still moves" rule.** No still sits at scale 1.0. Default to a push of 1.00→1.06-1.15 over the whole shot, linear or `sine.inOut`. On shots of 4-6 s use a two-speed move: fast for the first 40% (`power3.out`), then creep. For the 2.5D look, split foreground and background (AI depth or a manual cutout) and move FG 1.5× faster.
9. **Virtual-room portrait (Unreal substitute).** Generate an AI still of a dim period room: a sconce or candelabra in the foreground, the portrait frame on the wall. Then image-to-video with the prompt "slow dolly right, rack focus from lamp to portrait, shallow depth of field", 5 s. Or in HyperFrames: FG layer `filter: blur(0→8px)`, BG portrait `blur(10px→0)` over 1.2 s (`power2.inOut`), timed so the portrait goes sharp **0.3 s before the name is spoken**. Reuse the same room shot later (he reuses shot 7 at shot 16, 47 s apart). Budget 2-3 reusable room plates per character.
