## D. Patterns + recipes (for HyperFrames 1920×1080 @ 30 fps, TTS "Renee", whisper timing, SFX library, licensed beds)

### D1. Name-synced rack-focus reveal (3D-room photo)
Used: shots 5, 25 (and a slow version in 14).
- Build a "photo in a room" scene: the AI still inside a gilt frame `div`, a CSS 3D transform (`perspective: 1600px; rotateY(-8deg)`),
  a background plate (brick or wood still, blurred 6 px), and a warm key light done as a radial-gradient overlay (multiply, 0.55) plus a vignette.
- Timeline: cut in with `filter: blur(18px)` on the photo layer and `blur(10px)` on the wall. Hold the blur **0.8–2.2 s**, then tween
  to `blur(0)` over **0.6 s (snap) or 1.1 s (soft)** with `power2.out`. Use whisper timing to place the *end* of the pull
  **0–100 ms after the surname's onset**.
- Under the whole shot: `scale 1.00 → 1.00 + 0.016*dur` (≈1.6 %/s) with `ease: "none"`, plus `x: 0 → -16px/s`.
- Shot length 8–11 s. Never hold the blur past the second word after the name.

### D2. Silent black text card (plot beat the VO skips)
- Dip out: the previous shot's opacity 1 → 0 over **0.5 s (15 frames)** to `#000`.
- Text: small caps (`font-variant: small-caps` or `all-small-caps` on a humanist sans such as Optima, Gill Sans or Alegreya Sans SC),
  `letter-spacing: 0.15em`, colour `#E1E1E1`, capitals **2.8 % of frame height (30 px)**, centred, **two lines with a 12 % (130 px) baseline gap**,
  the block centred on y ≈ 51 %. Accent the two-word consequence in `#8B0A16`.
- Animate: line 1 `opacity 0→1, 0.8 s, sine.inOut`; line 2 starts 1.0 s later; hold until **≥ word_count / 3.5 s** of full legibility
  (22 words → ≈5–6 s); both lines fade out over 1.0 s; **0.6 s of pure black**, then a **hard cut to the brightest image you have**.
- Audio: **no VO**. Raise the music bed **+4 dB** for the card's length (ffmpeg `volume` automation), drop back at the hard cut. No SFX.

### D3. Year slam over a skyline
- A stock or AI skyline still, slow push of 0.5 %/s. Year in a white condensed heavy sans (Bebas Neue / Oswald 700), **33 % of frame height**,
  dead centre, static, no shadow. Show it **≈3 s before** the TTS says the year; cut away within 0.3 s after the year is spoken.

### D4. Archive-voice handover (borrowed authority) → J-cut expert
- When a "news" or "expert" voice carries a fact, mute the narrator. For us (no real archive): use a second TTS voice processed as broadcast
  (`highpass=f=300, lowpass=f=3400, acompressor`, −3 dB) over a 1990s-styled news card (a spinning newspaper in HyperFrames: scale 0.05→0.7,
  `rotation: 720 → 0`, 1.6 s, `expo.out`, plus 3 ghost copies at opacity 0.25 lagging 2 frames for the motion-trail look).
- J-cut: start the expert's audio **4.5 s before** showing them, over a related still (book page). Cut to the speaker 370 ms before
  their next phrase. Finish with a **1.0 s cross-dissolve** to their "credential" (book cover) while they finish the sentence.

### D5. Cut placement table (whisper words → cut times)
- Sentence start: cut = first word onset − **250 ms** (range 170–520; use 500 ms for the final or most important line).
- Name reveal: cut at the name onset (0 ms), or cut into blur 0.9 s early and resolve on the name (D1).
- List items: cut = word onset + **0–150 ms**.
- Chapter or section start: cut on the music re-entry, VO **0.9 s** later.
- Host/presenter inserts can cut mid-clause (+150 ms) because the voice carries continuity.

### D6. Pace curve for the last 75 s
- Scandal/action: median shot 4.7 s (archive-style 1.4–4 s).
- Coda: median **7 s**, one long contemplative symbolic shot of **14–16 s** about 30 s before the end, then a presenter beat of 7 s,
  then the emblem of 7 s.
- Keep every shot over 4 s alive: push 1.0–2.4 %/s, trucks of 15–20 px/s, particles (a canvas layer of 150–300 dust motes drifting 4–12 px/s,
  opacity 0.2–0.6), or focus changes.

### D7. Music map (ffmpeg)
- Bed level: speech −17 dB RMS, bed −28 dB under VO (**−11 dB** relative); sidechain or volume keyframes.
- VO gap with picture-only storytelling: bed **+4 to +5 dB**.
- Chapter breath: the bed dips to **−40 dB for ~3 s** after the chapter's last line; start a new cue/phrase **on the next cut**; VO in 0.9 s later.
- Place picture cuts at musical rests (find rests with `silencedetect=n=-38dB:d=0.4` on the music stem).
- Ending: final word → **2 s swell (+5 dB)** → cut to the logo → a fade of **8–9 s** (`afade=t=out:st=<logo_in>:d=8.5`), silence over the final 0.8 s.
  No stinger hit on the last word.

### D8. Bookend loop
- Build one hero emblem asset (our own original crest/logo/object) for the cold open. In the open, show it **dark, at the top edge**, and

  under the final line.
- Make the final line answer the intro's opening tension in one sentence of ≤12 words, ending on an emotion word.

### D9. Presenter moral beat
