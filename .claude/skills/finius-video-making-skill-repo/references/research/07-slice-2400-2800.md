## C. NUMBERS, CRISES AND INSTITUTIONS

**How figures appear: they don't.** In 240 s the narration says 37%, 1850s, 1870s, 1869, May 9 1893, 1893 (twice), late 1800s,
three decades, $65 million and 3.5 million ounces. **None of them is put on screen** as a card, counter, chart or headline [M, every shot checked].
Each number gets a *picture of what it means* instead:
- 37% of a portfolio → a map pin and flag over the country (the place, not the share).
- 1850s–1870s growth → rail lines drawing themselves across an antique US map.
- 1869 → cut into the portrait gallery on the year (the era turns).
- May 9 1893 → an anxious face, then a 4 s silent beat on flickering B/W crowd footage.
- Panic of 1893 → train over the lens, black, night fires, a white explosion flash on the year.
- "low on gold reserves" → the same gold-vault image **desaturated to grey and out of focus** (a colour metaphor for scarcity).
- "days away from depleting gold reserves" → gold bars on racks with **US-flag stripes behind** (gold + flag = state reserve).
- $65 million in bonds → a shadowy figure against a blown-out window (secrecy, not size).
- 3.5 million ounces → a 3D **handover of gold bars between two top-hatted men** with a slow 1.24x push. One staged exchange stands in for the whole deal.

**Institutions** are shown as rooms, buildings and portraits, never as logos:
- the presidency → a 3D Oval-Office-like room with the president's B/W portrait framed on the wall (flag, bookcase, warm practicals).
  It is reused 4 times (wide → CU → wide → CU), each with a push or pull of 1.10–1.22.
- the industrial titans → a **3D portrait gallery**: 3 frames on dark wood, lit one by one as they are named.
- Wall Street, the Bank of England and the City → building exteriors (a grey corner building with a flag, a London facade with carriages).
- the House of Morgan → an archive photo treated as a scratched glass plate, plus a backlit drama actor (a stand-in, never named on screen).
- the Rothschilds → a European council room with god rays and firelight, and later gold mining.
- a source or expert → a historian's photo and book as props on a desk (citation by object).

**How the panic is staged**: the pace speeds up just before it (a 1.0 / 0.92 s burst on train and wheels), then cuts to dark at the moment of collapse.
Then night, fire, riot silhouettes, and an explosion flash on the year. Colour goes sepia → black → orange fire → white flash.
Sound: the bed is pulled to −30 dBFS, with long silent beats after the date. Straight after the panic the edit **cools down**: the 3D presidential room
with steady 3–7 s pushes and the presenter on camera (10.5 s) explaining the gold standard.

Hold place A for 1.9 s with scale 1.00→1.24 (`power1.inOut`). Whip: animate x by −1.5 frame widths over 15 frames (0.5 s, `power3.inOut`),

Land on place B, then pull out 1.0→0.88 over 4 s so both pins are in view. Labels fade in 8 frames after landing.

**D3. Archive-photo-as-object**: put any portrait (public domain or generated) into a frame inside a room, or onto a scratched glass plate
(scratch PNG overlay in screen mode at 30%, vignette at 40%). Always move it: push 1.08–1.24 over the shot length (scale per second ≈ 0.03–0.06),
plus a 20–40 px tilt. Never hold a still flat on screen.

**D4. Spoken numbers, shown meanings**: in story sections, **do not card every number**. For each figure, write a "what it means" shot:
a scarcity number gets a desaturated version of the asset (`filter: saturate(0) blur(6px)`, the same shot used saturated earlier);
a deal amount gets a staged handover close-up with a 1.24x push over 7–8 s; a date gets a reaction face plus a 3–4 s silent beat.
Keep stat cards for explainer sections (other slices) and keep them to one per 60 s at most.

**D5. Crisis sequence (20–25 s)**: (1) 2–3 shots of the boom at 2–4 s each; (2) a burst of 2–3 shots at 0.9–1.3 s;
(3) a "collapse" shot that ends in black (a 0.6 s fade to #000, or an object passing the lens) **on the verb**;
(4) 2–3 dark night shots at 1.5–3 s with an orange accent; (5) a white flash on the crisis name or year:
a white solid with opacity 0→0.9 over 3 frames, back to 0 over 10 frames, plus an impact or boom SFX at −8 dB;
(6) duck the music bed by a further 6 dB (gaps reading about −30 dBFS) for the whole sequence; (7) after the date sentence, leave 3.5–4 s with no narration
(music only, a 5 Hz ticking texture, a low swell +4 dB at 2.5 s), over grainy flickering footage:
CSS brightness keyframes switching between 0.85 and 1.05 every 2 frames, plus grain.

**D6. Chapter button**: end the chapter's final sentence on an emblem shot (a flag or skyline) with a 0.82x pull-out over 5.5 s.
Let the music run 3–4 s after the last word, then hard-cut the music to silence for 300 ms (a gain automation step in the ffmpeg mix, `volume=0` between two timestamps).
Start the next section's picture 0.4 s before its first word, on a bed with less bass (EQ at −10 dB below 150 Hz for the first 10–20 s).

**D7. Name-reveal silence**: end a sentence on a key name, then drop the whole mix to about −35 dBFS (music off; keep room tone) for 4 s.
Cut to the next subject's B-roll 2.4 s into the silence, and bring the voice and a new bed in together 1.8 s after that cut. Use it once per 5–10 minutes at most.

**D8. Cut placement defaults (whisper word times)**: B-roll sentence cut = first-word onset − 170 ms (range 0 to −470).
Noun insert = noun onset + 150 ms. Presenter or on-camera beat = start the sentence over B-roll and cut in 1.1–1.4 s later.
Interviewee or quote = J-cut, with the voice starting 4–5 s before the face; cut back to B-roll for the last third of the quote.

**D9. Pacing envelope**: narrative sections run at a 2.4–3 s median shot length; explainer sections at a 4.6–4.8 s median, made of presenter
or talking-head shots of 8–10 s with 3D push shots in between. Never leave a still image on screen longer than 4 s without at least a
1.08x push or a 100 px drift.
In HyperFrames, give every still a default `gsap.fromTo(img,{scale:1},{scale:1+0.04*dur, ease:"none"})` and alternate push and pull between shots that cut together.

**D10. One-frame dip to black**: when moving from the presenter to dramatic B-roll, put a single black frame (33 ms at 30 fps) between them. It reads as a hard reset with no visible effect.

**Caveats**: VTT word onsets after long pauses are unreliable (the VTT put "1893" at 1544.9, but the audio shows it at about 1540.5; I used the audio and transcript.txt).
The SFX layer is hard to separate from the narration in a mono mix. Only the map whoosh and the 5 Hz pause texture are clearly measurable.
