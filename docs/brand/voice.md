# GSWAU voice guide

Every guide, page and post written for GSWAU follows these rules. The `/new-guide` command
reads this file before it writes anything, and checks the finished page against it.

## Tone

- Professional, direct and honest.
- On the barber's side. Write like someone who wants the reader to keep more of their money.
- Say the answer first, then explain it. Short sentences. Plain words.

## What GSWAU stands for

- **The goal:** valuable information without wasting money. Step by step, from the right people.
- **Against:** garbage information online, products disguised as education, and wasting money.
- Never push a purchase as the only way forward. The free information on the page must be useful
  on its own. Courses and membership are offered as the next step, not as the answer.

## Who we write for

Barbers and beauticians:

- **Newcomers** who lack business knowledge and need the basics laid out clearly.
- **Veterans** who want a way up (more income, a better setup) or a way out (owning, teaching,
  leaving the chair).

Write so both can use the same page: explain any term a newcomer may not know, and do not
pad the page with things a veteran already knows.

## Style rules

- No hype. No "game-changer", "secret", "level up", "crush it", "skyrocket" or similar.
- No slang such as "fam", "bro" or "king".
- Minimal emoji in general, and **no emoji on guide pages**.
- The author byline is always **Gs. W. Augustin**, spelled exactly like that.

## Facts and numbers

- **Never invent** statistics, studies, survey results, quotes, testimonials, prices of other
  businesses, or facts about Gs. W. Augustin.
- Worked examples use simple round numbers and are clearly labelled **"Example only"**.
- If a point needs a real number that has not been given (an average price, a percentage, a
  legal or tax rule), leave it out of the page or write it in general terms, and **list it in
  the pull request** under "Claims to double-check".
- Tax, legal and licensing topics: give the general idea and tell the reader to check with a
  local accountant or their licensing board. Do not state rules for a specific country or state
  unless they have been given.

## Never

- Never mention anything about Gs. W. Augustin's personal background.
- Never name or criticise a real competitor, person or brand.
- Never promise results ("you will double your income").

## Quick check before publishing

- [ ] The short answer actually answers the question in 2–4 sentences.
- [ ] No invented numbers, studies, quotes or testimonials.
- [ ] Every example number is labelled as an example.
- [ ] No hype words, no slang, no emoji.
- [ ] Nothing about Gs. W. Augustin's personal background.
- [ ] The page is useful even if the reader buys nothing.
